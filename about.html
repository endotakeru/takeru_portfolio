Key Projects

Cart-Pole Single Inverted Pendulum: Controls, Simulation & Embedded Systems
May – Jun 2026
Self-balancing cart-pole control system
Urbana-Champaign, IL
  • Derived the cart-pole's nonlinear dynamics via Lagrange's method and linearized to a state-space model, exposing an unstable +7.19 rad/s right-half-plane pole and setting dual-loop PD gains analytically from a target damping ratio and natural frequency.
  • Programmed a 200 Hz real-time PD controller in C++ on an Arduino Uno, fusing a 10-bit P3022 angle encoder, 6× oversampled, with stepper step-count dead-reckoning to drive a DRV8825 / NEMA 17 actuator, recovering ±7° tilts and settling to ±0.5° in ~0.7 s.
  • Fabricated the rig from Fusion 360 CAD: 3D-printed PLA on a 0.540 m aluminum T-slot rail with GT2 belt drive, plus a KiCad circuit with a 72.3 Hz RC anti-alias filter sized to the 100 Hz Nyquist limit, then validated it against a SciPy RK45 nonlinear simulation with ±15° recovery.
